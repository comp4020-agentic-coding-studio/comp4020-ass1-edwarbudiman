Six decks · Dealer stands on all 17s · Hit and stand only · No double, no split

You have sixteen and the dealer is showing a ten. Every choice from here is a choice between two bad options — and this page is about how you tell which one is better.

Reference
Shoe
The finite collection of cards a Finite Shoe deals from. Has a size, a remaining count, and a composition.
Upcard
The one dealer card shown face up before you decide. The dealer's second card stays hidden until your hand is settled.
Push
A Play-out that settles even: your total and the dealer's tie, and nothing changes hands. A push proves the Decision no more right than a loss would prove it wrong.
Draw
A single next card, considered on its own — what the Act 1 strip shows for all thirteen possible next cards against your hand.
Play-out
One hand carried from your Decision through the dealer's turn to settlement. Answers what the Decision paid, not what the next card was.
Running Count
The Hi-Lo tally of cards that have left the Shoe: low cards raise it, tens and aces lower it. Describes what remains in the Shoe, never what comes next.
